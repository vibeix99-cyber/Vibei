## 1. Root cause and architecture: agreed

- The diagnosis is right. On the base every placement is a proxy (`toastLiftBottom` above `[data-toast-above]`, `toastRoomTop`/`topBarBottom` under a `<header>`, `toastDock` only side by side) and nothing checks the invariant. Every past toast defect is a geometry the proxy did not foresee: D4 (toast over "Mark it done"), R5-D1/LEAD-D1 (list states pushing a box out of its room), R7-D1 (dock shrinks the room below MIN), round-6 #8 and (e) (lift lands on the readout), R8-F3 (stack above the Ambience sheet), W2H-D1 (a taller dock lifts the stack onto Mark done), (d) (fold hides icon and amount).
- Zones + a measured protected set + bands + a queue + announcement decoupled from drawing is the right shape: the invariant is checked against the thing itself, and a layout change can only change *how many* notices fit, never *what* they cover. Approved.
- Hypothesis for (b) **confirmed in code and in the BEFORE evidence.** `FocusScreen.tsx` l.~482: `onEnd={() => { setEndOpen(false); endFocusEarly(); }}` — both in one handler, batched into one render in which `view` is already `none`, so the component returns `frozen.current`, the *previous* element tree, which still carries `<EndSheet open>`; the portalled `Sheet` stays present (`aria-modal="true"`, `#root` **inert**) for the outgoing layer's life. BEFORE meta, e.g. `390x844-light-t100/03-today-after-end-0.6s`: hash `#/`, `dialog: "Leave the kettle early? …"` still present, Mark done's centre hit-tests to `div._footer_4dq2b_142` (the sheet footer) and the clear button to `div._header_4dq2b_94`; gone by 1.5 s there, up to 14.6 s under load in W2-H's F3. ### Decision 2 — notices wait when no strip is free
- **ACCEPTED as a bounded rule,** not an open-ended one. Waiting is permitted only in three states: (i) a modal is open or exiting; (ii) the zone has been scrolled off screen by the person; (iii) the summary card fills the stage at 200 %. **A notice must never wait on a screen at rest** (scroll 0, no sheet) at any configuration. This closes a hole in the note: Settings, Stats, Nook and Welcome "use the viewport as their zone", and a Settings page whose viewport is full of rows (controls) has no band ≥ MIN, so the backup toasts would wait forever. Those screens **declare a zone** too (the `ScreenHeader` title block is the natural one; T chooses) or T proves a band at rest at every configuration at 100 and 200 %.
- **No viewport fallback on a screen that has a zone.** Rule 3's last bullet ("If no zone has such a band, the viewport is tried") contradicts rule 4 ("If there is no band at all, notices wait") and reintroduces exactly what T rejected ("land on readouts and summary text and jump about"). On Today scrolled, the viewport is all controls and unmarked facts (recipes, today's brews); on the session screen it is the panel's text. Zone screens: a band in the zone, or wait.
- **Does a late notice lose meaning? No, under these conditions:** timers run only while drawn (an Undo window is never spent waiting); each notice is announced once on arrival, with its action named when it has one ("Crossed off. Nice work. Undo"); nothing is dropped by a route change (a waiting notice is drawn on the next screen that has a band); "Saved N minutes" and reward notices are facts about a past event and stay true; the update prompt stays valid.
- **The persistent update prompt must not starve the queue.** With "action notices first" and a band that holds one notice (375×667 @200), `A fresh brew of Kettle is ready · Update` (`duration: Infinity`) would hold the only slot and every timed notice would wait until the person acts. Ruling: **a persistent notice yields to a waiting timed notice and returns when a band frees;** it loses nothing by yielding. Priority otherwise: timed action notices (Undo) at the head of the queue, then arrival order.
- **Drawn notices are never withdrawn by a newcomer** (the LEAD-D1 lesson: no reshuffle under the finger). A newcomer that does not fit waits. A notice withdrawn because its band vanished (scroll, sheet, resize) **returns only into a band that holds it whole** (hysteresis), so no withdraw/re-draw flicker at a threshold.
- The removal of the three-toast store cap is accepted (it lost facts); T shows the worst weekday's drain time at 375×667 @200 (§4.4).
- Existing `DataSection.tsx` l.68 already dismisses toasts before its import sheet opens; T lists every toast that can arrive while a modal is open (End's two, a break ending under the Ambience sheet, "Backup restored · Undo", the update prompt) and shows what is drawn when the modal closes.

## 3. Further rulings on the note (binding in implementation)

1. **Rule 2(c) selector.** `[role=dialog][aria-modal]` does **not** match an exiting sheet: `Sheet.tsx` sets `aria-modal={isPresent ? 'true' : undefined}` and the layer gets `inert` while it animates out. Use `[role="dialog"]` presence (any) for "including while it animates out", and treat `#root[inert]` as "modal open" too.
2. **Exiting route layers.** `App.tsx` `TabSwitch` uses `AnimatePresence mode="popLayout"`: the outgoing screen stays in the DOM, with its own zone, for the exit's duration. A zone in an exiting layer is **never used** (the stack must not open in the session's stage and jump to Today's Hero). Protected boxes everywhere are still honoured. The mechanism is T's choice (e.g. only zones inside the layer whose screen matches the current route); the proof is the W2H-D1 trace: the stack's box from its first sample shows no jump beyond the entry motion.
3. **Measurement cadence.** ResizeObserver does not see position changes (a sticky dock settling, popLayout transforms, the "Carried from your last brew · Mark done" row appearing). Re-measure every frame while a route transition or layout animation runs, or keep a short interval as the base does; the invariant is sampled at ≤ 100 ms in the proofs anyway.
4. **Clipping.** The region's box is the band and clips the list: entry/exit motion must stay inside it, and the `layout` animation must not clip a whole notice for longer than R8-F1's base figure (≤ 0.5 s). Shadows may clip.
5. **Announcer.** One polite announcement per notice on arrival, message plus action label; zero further live-region mutations when the notice is drawn, withdrawn, re-drawn or leaves (count them, as R8 did). The drawn list keeps `aria-label="Notifications"`, is not a live region, and its actions stay in the tab order.
6. **Protected set (a).** Keep `disabled` controls protected (they are visible). Controls under the opaque dock are over-protected; accept. The 6 px slop is fine.
7. **Protected text (b).** Add the goal card's text (ruling above). The session readout's `data-notice-avoid` must cover the flag, digits, status line and meta at every view (focus, paused, break, whistle), the top bar's chip and cycle dots, the Break's over title and sub, and the storage banner via `[data-save-warning]` (not edited).
8. **Reduced motion.** As the note says; proven at the same configurations with `--rm`.
9. **Scope.** No `screens/done/**`, no `scene/**`, no art, no new dependencies, no edit to the frozen `integrity.spec.ts` assertions (waits may be made deterministic on real state, never weakened).
