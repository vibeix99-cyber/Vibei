Grand Line Arena

A tactical turn-based PvP fighter battler in the Pokémon lineage, built in Three.js. Every piece below is built by its own agent, then judged by a fresh critic that drives the actual running build — never the builder's write-up — and compares it blind against the real Pokémon games. A piece that loses gets sent back with its single biggest gap named.
